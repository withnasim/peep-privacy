Peep – My Little Talking Chick: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Peep – My Little Talking Chick ("the app") is a virtual pet game for all ages. You can talk to Peep, feed her, give her a bath, dress her up and take her to different places. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. The app does not have internet access: it does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Microphone

The app asks for permission to use the microphone so Peep can hear you and repeat what you say in her chick voice. When the microphone is on, the sound is processed in real time on your device only: Peep listens, changes your voice into her voice and plays it back.

  • Your voice is never recorded to a file, saved or stored.
  • Your voice is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The microphone stops whenever the app is closed or in the background.
  • You can turn the microphone off at any time with the mic button at the top of the screen, or deny the permission in Android settings. Everything else in the app works without it.

Data stored on your device

Peep's outfit, her current place, when she hatched and how happy and full she is are saved only on your device, so she is the same the next time you play. This data never leaves your device. The app turns off Android's app-data backup.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of the app's data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is designed to be suitable for children and families. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat or social features and no links to websites or other apps. The microphone is used only as described above, on the device, and nothing is recorded or shared.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
